
# FPGA FM Demodulator

Real-time FM demodulation on an FPGA at 100 MHz. IQ samples from an RTL-SDR are streamed from a PC over UART (5 Mbaud), demodulated with a CORDIC phase detector, low-pass filtered/decimated with a FIR, and sent back to the PC as audio. Tested on a Basys 3.

## Design Notes
At 5 Mbaud, with 20 bits per SDR I/Q sample, the Basys receives samples at a
 rate of 250 kS/s. After demodulation, the datastream is passed through a 93 tap symmetric FIR filter with a cutoff of 25 kHz and decimated by 5, sending ~50 kS/s audio back to the PC. Filter taps were generated by scripts/tap_gen.py, and use a Q1.15 fixed point format.
